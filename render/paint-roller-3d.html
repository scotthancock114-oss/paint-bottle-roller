  • Base, corner rails, lid fixed; shown see-through so the drive is visible
  • Level modules 90 mm each (up to 3), 6 bottles, carried at all four corners; about 49 mm of hand room above the bottles
  • End lifters 4 bars, one segment per level; the two ends rise and fall in opposition
  • Rollers 4 per deck along the bottles, 29 mm pitch
  • One roll motor on level 1 spare Ender 3 NEMA 17; GT2 220 belts carry drive up; O-rings link each level's rollers
  • Bumpers + cranks open front and cap end for loading by hand; one rock motor, two cranks 180° apart

Concept model built from the design dimensions, not from CAD. Motion is sped up and simplified. There is no centre pivot: each deck hangs from lifter bars at both ends, and one crankshaft in the base raises one end while it lowers the other. Every level rides the same bars, so each gets the same end lift and angle, and the rock clearance is needed only below the first level and under the lid. The front of each level is open with only a low lip, so you reach in from the front or the cap end and lift bottles straight out.
